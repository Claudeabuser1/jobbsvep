Matchd – Chrome extension

Install (once):
1. Unzip this folder somewhere you will keep it (e.g. Documents/jobbsvep-extension).
2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the folder.
5. Pin "Matchd" from the puzzle-piece menu.

Use:
- Open Matchd in a Chrome tab once so the extension can pick up your name,
  contact details and drafted applications. (Or use "Kopiera mina uppgifter
  till tillägget" in the app and paste it in the extension.)
- In the extension, open "Mina uppgifter" and add your address and CV file.
- On an employer's application form: click the extension, pick the
  application, press "Fyll i formuläret". Check everything, then submit yourself.
- On a job ad (LinkedIn, Instagram, anywhere): "Kopiera annonsen på den här
  sidan", then paste it under "Importera en annons" in Matchd.

The extension never submits a form, never ticks checkboxes and never fills
personal identity numbers or passwords. Everything is stored in this Chrome
profile only.
