# Short videos and a new name

## 1. What the "very Chinese time in my life" videos are

- **Origin:** a parody of the *Fight Club* line "You met me at a very strange time in my life", with "strange" swapped for "Chinese". It is usually traced to a post on X in April 2025 and spread on TikTok and Instagram in late 2025 and early 2026. Know Your Meme calls the phrase "purposefully absurd".
- **Format:** a short vertical video of everyday clips: warm water, slippers indoors, a rice cooker, tai chi, dim sum. The caption "You met me at a very Chinese time in my life" sits on top, usually over a trending sound. Hashtags include #verychinesetime and #becomingchinese. The trend is also called "Chinamaxxing".
- **Criticism:** several outlets (ABC News, Coveteur, Vogue Singapore) point out that most people posting it aren't Chinese, and that it can turn a culture into an aesthetic.

**What to copy for Jobbsvep:** the **sentence template**, not the Chinese part. "You met me at a very ___ time in my life" works with anything, and job hunting at 17 is relatable, funny and on-brand. It also keeps you clear of the cultural criticism.

## 2. How to make one (about 20 minutes)

1. **Film 4–6 clips of 1–3 seconds each,** vertical (9:16), in daylight. Phone camera is fine.
2. **Edit in CapCut** (free) or TikTok's built-in editor:
   - New project, add the clips in order, and cut each to 1–2 seconds.
   - Add the caption with the Text tool. Place it in the top third so the app's buttons don't cover it. Keep it on screen for the whole video.
   - Optional: a second text at the end with the punchline and "jobbsvep.se".
3. **Pick the sound inside TikTok or Instagram,** not in CapCut. Business accounts on TikTok can only use the **Commercial Music Library**. Popular songs can get your video muted or removed. Search the library for a calm, trending sound.
4. **Post with 3–5 hashtags.** Swedish: #sommarjobb #extrajobb #förstajobbet #jobbsökning #jobbsvep. English: #firstjob #jobhunting #studentjob #jobsearch.
5. **Post at the right times:** about three times a week, between 15:00 and 18:00 on weekdays, when school is out.

The hook must land in the first 1–2 seconds. Start with the funniest clip, not the logo.

## 4. Rules so the videos help instead of hurt

- **No made-up results.** Only say "got a job" if it really happened. Don't show fake reviews or fake numbers.
- **Consent.** Ask before you film staff or customers in a shop or café, or film only yourself.
- **Ads aimed at minors.** In the EU you may not show ads to under-18s based on profiling (the Digital Services Act). Normal posts are fine. If you pay for ads, target 18+ or use broad targeting without profiling, and read the platform's rules.
- **Be honest about the app.** Show the real app, real swipes, real letters. Blur personal details such as your phone number and address.

## 5. A new name that works internationally

What a good name needs here:

- Short and easy to say in Swedish and English.
- Hints at swiping or work.
- Free as both `.com` and `.se`.
- Not confusingly close to an existing company.

I checked domains on 14 October 2026 with `whois`. A free domain can be taken any day, so register the one you choose soon. I also searched for existing companies with similar names. That search is not a full trademark check; before you commit, search the Swedish (PRV) and EU (EUIPO) trademark registers.

| Name | .com | .se | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hejshift** | free | free | **My pick.** "Hej" is Swedish and instantly friendly, "shift" means a work shift in English. Works as "Hejshift" internationally and still feels Swedish. No company found with this name. |
| **Jobbsvep** | free | free | Keep it for Sweden only: perfect in Swedish, hard to say for everyone else. Good if you launch other countries under local names. |
| **Swipe for Work** | free | free | Says exactly what it does in English. Long, and less of a brand. |
| Hejjob / Heyjobb / Jobhej | free | free | **Avoid.** Too close to **HeyJobs**, an established German recruiting platform, which is a trademark risk. |
| Swiply, Shiftswipe, Gigswipe, Swipr, Joblio, Heyshift, Firstswipe, Workswipe, Swipeshift | taken | free | Only `.se` is free, so the name can't go international. |
| Swipejob, Jobswipe, Swipehire, Firstshift, Jobbly, Applyo, Snapjob | taken | taken | Taken. |

**Suggestion:** use **Hejshift** as the brand (`hejshift.com` and `hejshift.se`), and keep `jobbsvep.se` as a Swedish address that forwards to it. Decide before you buy the domain, set up social accounts, or publish the app, because the app ID can never change after the first upload.
